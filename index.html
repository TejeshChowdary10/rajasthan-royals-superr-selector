Pressure Index (PI) & Pressure Reliability Index (PRI)

Innings 2: Chase Leverage Formulation

Every delivery in a chase is evaluated dynamically on four mechanical parameters:

  • Required Run Rate (p_RRR): clip((RRR - 5.0) / 9.0, 0.1, 1.0).
  • Resource Scarcity (p_wickets): (10 - W_rem) / 9.0.
  • Time Urgency (p_urgency): 0.30 + 0.70 × (120 - B_rem) / 120.
  • Contest Viability Filter: Chases requiring > 4 runs/ball with > 6 balls left collapse to dead time (x0.40).
PI_chase = 0.45 * p_RRR + 0.30 * p_urgency + 0.25 * p_wickets

Innings 1: Target Setting & Crisis Recovery

First-innings pressure reflects structural crisis and finishing leverage:

  • Top-Order Collapse: Wickets ≥ 2 in Powerplay or ≥ 4 before over 12 triggers crisis multiplier.
  • Death Phase Acceleration: Overs 16–20 carry par-setting leverage (0.65 to 0.85).
  • Playoffs Multiplier: Knockout matches scaled by 1.15x.
  • Super Overs: Maximal pressure (PI = 1.00).
PI_first = clip(p_phase + p_crisis, 0.25, 0.95) * k_knockout

The Four Pillars of PRI Scoring

PRI combines Absolute Execution (35%, shrunk via Empirical Bayes), Clutch Differential vs Baseline (20%, Δ SR and inflation-resistant Δ Econ), Contextual Runs Added/Saved (25%, ball-by-ball expectation relative to phase), and Match-Level Consistency (20%, positive contribution rate across high-pressure matches) scaled by logarithmic volume mastery.
